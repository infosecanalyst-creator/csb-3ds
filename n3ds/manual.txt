# Welcome
Dungeon Master for the New 3DS plays the original Dungeon Master and Chaos Strikes Back with CSBwin, a faithful remake of the original engine.

You lead up to four champions through a real-time dungeon. Monsters move while you think, food and water run down, and torches burn out. The game never pauses unless you freeze it (START) or open this manual.

## Reading this manual
- Circle Pad or C-Stick: scroll the text (hold to keep scrolling).
- D-pad: move between the section buttons on the touch screen. A section opens as you pick it; on CLOSE, press A.
- L and R: previous and next section.
- Tap a section to open it.
- B or START (or tap CLOSE): close. The manual opens again where you left it.
- Open it from the HELP tab in the dungeon, or with SELECT on any menu screen.

# Getting started
## Installing over Wi-Fi
1. On the 3DS, open the Homebrew Launcher and start ftpd.
2. Upload csb3ds.3dsx and csb.cfg to sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/.
3. Close ftpd and start Dungeon Master from the Homebrew Launcher.

Updates install the same way. You never need to take the SD card out.

## Game files
The game needs GRAPHICS.DAT and DUNGEON.DAT from Dungeon Master (or Chaos Strikes Back) in sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/. No game data comes with this port.

## Starting
The first screen is the game's own menu. Tap DUNGEON for a new game, or pick a saved game. At the entrance, tap ENTER.

## Saves
Save from the disk icon in any champion's inventory. Saves are written to sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/.

# The two screens
## Bottom screen: the dungeon
The touch screen is where you play. Use the stylus like a mouse; a finger works too.
- Across the top: each champion's box with their name, both hands and three bars. Tap the name to make them the leader, a hand to take or place the item in it, or the bars to open their inventory. Anywhere in the box counts, so a finger is enough.
- The dungeon view: tap to pick items up off the floor, push wall buttons, open doors and put things in alcoves.
- With an item in hand, tap the upper half of the view to throw it, or tap STOW (the BAG tab) to put it in the leader's backpack.

## The tabs
| FIGHT | Attack menu. Tap a weapon, then an attack.
| SPELL | Rune panel for casting.
| RECAST | Casts the last spell again. Hold it to pick any champion's last spell.
| BAG | The leader's inventory. With an item in hand it reads STOW and puts the item in the leader's backpack.
| SWAP | Moves the big view to the top screen (look only: tap SWAP again, or press ZL, to touch the dungeon).
| HELP | This manual.

## Top screen: information
- Party: each champion's button (A, B, X or Y; lit when their weapon is ready, grey while it recovers), name, LEAD for the leader, HP, STA and MP with current/max (stamina is shown divided by ten, as in the game), FOOD and WATER, and POISON or DEAD.
- Map: every square you have seen.
- LIGHT: how bright your torches and spells keep the dungeon.
- Messages: the last lines the game printed. Hold ZR to see the whole log.

## SWAP
SWAP (or ZL, any time in the dungeon) puts the dungeon view full-size on the top screen, with 3D depth from the 3D slider, and moves the map to the bottom. The top screen can't be touched, so to pick things up or press buttons, tap SWAP again or press ZL. The game remembers your choice.

# Controls
| D-pad | Step forward or back, turn left or right
| Circle Pad | Same as the D-pad
| L / R | Step sideways
| A B X Y | Attack with champion 1 2 3 4 (left to right on the portraits; hold to keep attacking)
| ZR + A B X Y | That champion's second attack (the third is on the FIGHT panel)
| ZR + tap | Right click
| ZR (hold) | Show the whole message log
| ZL | Swap the screens (dungeon)
| SELECT | Inventory in the dungeon; manual on menus
| START | Freeze or unfreeze the game
| START + SELECT | Quit (asks first)
| Left hand | The LEFT HAND button on the manual's touch screen swaps the D-pad with Y, A, B and X (Y is up, A is right, B is down, X is left, and the reverse), so the stylus can be held in the left hand. It is remembered.
| 3D slider | Depth for the top-screen view and map

The A B X Y attacks and SELECT inventory only work in the dungeon, so they can never press a menu button by accident.

# Champions
The Hall of Champions, just inside the entrance, has mirrors. Tap a mirror to see the champion inside.
- RESURRECT brings them back as they were, with their name and skills.
- REINCARNATE gives them a new name. They lose their skill levels but gain extra strength and other stats in return.
- CANCEL leaves them in the mirror.

You can carry four champions. Their colour (green, yellow, red, blue) is the same on the portraits, the bars and the messages.

## The leader
The leader carries what is in your hand and leads the party; LEAD on the top screen shows who it is. Tap a champion's name on their portrait to make them the leader.

# Fighting
Monsters attack from the front, and sometimes the sides. Only the two champions at the front can hit with most weapons. The two at the back can throw things, fire bows or cast spells.

## Attacking
- Press A, B, X or Y. That champion uses their first attack. The letters are shown beside the names on the top screen.
- Hold the button to keep attacking whenever their weapon is ready.
- For a different attack, hold ZR (second) with the button, or tap FIGHT, tap the weapon and choose. The FIGHT panel closes by itself after the attack.

After an attack, the champion's letter on the top screen turns grey until they recover.

## Damage
Damage flashes on the champion's portrait, and their HP bar on the top screen drops. If HP turns red, back off or drink a healing potion. A dead champion's bones can be carried back to a resurrection altar.

# Spells
Tap SPELL to open the rune panel. Every spell is a row of runes:
1. A power rune first: LO, UM, ON, EE, PAL or MON. More power is stronger and costs more mana.
2. Then one to three more runes. The panel shows the next set of runes after each tap.
3. Tap the line of runes you have entered to cast.

The arrow at the bottom right removes the last rune. Tap a champion's name tab at the top of the panel to choose who casts.

## Some first spells
Each starts with a power rune; LO is the cheapest.
| LO FUL | Magic torch (light)
| LO OH IR RA | Longer-lasting light
| LO FUL IR | Fireball
| LO VI | Healing potion (hold an empty flask)
| LO YA | Stamina potion (hold an empty flask)
| LO ZO | Opens some doors

There is no healing spell: healing comes from VI potions.

RECAST repeats the last spell, with the same caster and runes, in one tap. Hold RECAST for a moment and a button appears for each champion with a remembered spell; slide to one and let go to cast theirs. Any runes already typed are cleared first.

# Inventory
Tap a portrait, the BAG tab or SELECT to open the inventory. The dungeon view is replaced by that champion's equipment and backpack, at a larger size for fingers. The top screen keeps showing the map and the party.
- Tap an item to pick it up, then tap a slot to put it down.
- Eat and drink: double-tap the food or the flask. An empty flask goes back where it was. (Or pick it up and tap the mouth.)
- The eye icon shows details of the item you hold, or the champion's stats when your hand is empty.
- The disk icon saves the game. ZZZ puts the party to sleep to recover.
- SELECT, BAG or the portrait again closes the inventory.

# The map
The map on the top screen draws every square you have seen. North is always up.
- Solid lines are walls. Dotted lines are open edges you can walk across.
- The white arrow with the red tip is the party, pointing the way you face.
- Orange bars are doors, stripes are stairs and dark squares are pits.
- The level number is top left, your facing top right.

The map starts empty each time you start the game.

# Survival
- Food and water: watch the FOOD and WATER bars. They turn yellow, then red. Eat and drink before they run out.
- Light: torches burn down. When LIGHT drops, light a new torch or cast FUL.
- Rest: sleep (ZZZ in the inventory) to recover HP, stamina and mana. Monsters can still find you.
- Save often. The dungeon does not forgive.
- Freeze with START any time you need to stop.

# Troubleshooting
## There is no sound
Homebrew needs a copy of the 3DS sound firmware. Run DSP1 from the Homebrew Launcher once, then start the game again.

## It says the game data is missing
Put GRAPHICS.DAT and DUNGEON.DAT in sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/.

## A tap does nothing
Some screens zoom in on their buttons. Hold ZR on a menu screen to see the whole screen.

## Debugging
An empty file named debug.txt in sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/ writes csb3ds.log there and marks each tap with a crosshair.

## Changing this manual
A file named manual.txt in sdmc:/3ds/csb3ds/ replaces the built-in text. Sections start with "# ", headings with "## ", list items with "- ", numbered steps with "1. " and table rows with "| ".
